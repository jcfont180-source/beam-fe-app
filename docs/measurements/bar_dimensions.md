# Test bar measured dimensions

## Bar

- MOKOVI 6061-T6511 aluminum flat bar, nominal 1/8" x 1" x 12"
  (3.175 x 25.4 x 305 mm)
- E = 68,900 MPa (nominal for 6061-T6)

## Instrument

- Digital calipers, 0-150 mm, 0.01 mm resolution
- Zero check (jaws fully closed): 0.00 mm
- Date measured: October 11, 2026

## Method

Bar clamped flat to the desk under a wood block, measuring spot
overhanging the edge 3-5 cm, forearms braced, jaws closed gently, lock
screw engaged, caliper slid off and read stationary.

Stations measured from the end marked with marker.

### Rejected reading

Width at 150 mm was first read as 25.06 mm, about 0.14 mm below the
other stations. It was re-measured as 25.25 mm, and that value is used.
The rejected reading is kept here for the record and is not used in
any calculation.
